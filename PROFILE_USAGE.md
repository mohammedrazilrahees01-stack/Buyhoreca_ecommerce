# Company profile usage

The supplied BuyHoreca and Blue Mountain company profiles are kept in `docs/company-profiles/` as reference material only.

They are **not rendered as PDF pages or page screenshots in the storefront**.

The storefront uses selected factual details from the profiles as normal web content. It uses the existing BuyHoreca site imagery and brand assets for presentation instead of placing profile-page screenshots into the UI.

The header/footer use the actual BuyHoreca logo asset at `public/images/buyhoreca-logo-header.png`.

Homepage blog rail:
- 2 cards visible on mobile
- 3 cards on tablet
- 4 cards on desktop
- automatically advances horizontally
- pauses while the pointer is hovering over the rail
- resumes after hover leaves
